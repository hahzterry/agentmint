## What makes it special? (under 500 characters)

AgentMint turns AI agents into ownable, revenue-generating Solana assets. Describe an agent in plain English, test it in a live chat, then launch its token through Bags with fee sharing configured automatically. Every trade pays the creator, and forks route a share back to the original builder. Agents can use live crypto data, web search, calculations, token analytics, and even call other agents.

## First comment

Most AI-agent builders stop at deployment, while token launchpads stop at speculation. We built AgentMint to connect creation, utility, ownership, and revenue: AI generates the agent, users test it, Bags launches the token, and trading fees reward creators. Fork attribution adds a composable royalty layer so useful agents can become small on-chain businesses.

## Why we built it

Useful AI agents are difficult to monetize, while most launched tokens have little utility. AgentMint joins the two: an agent has a working product from day one, its creator earns from trading activity, and derivative agents continue rewarding the original builder.
